You’re sitting there in the waning days of August, staring at a roster you spent three months assembling like a frightened toddler building a house of cards, talking about your *long-term asset allocation*. You’re talking about flexibility. You’re talking about a youth movement.

I’m talking about backbone. And you ain’t got none.

I told you in August. I’ll tell you again right now before a single Sunday boot hits a leather pigskin. A rebuild is a coward’s word for quitting. Anyone who punts a season before Week 1 is stealing from the eleven men who actually showed up to play this game to win. You think you’re being clever. You think stockpiling picks is some kind of masterclass in modern dynasty architecture. It’s not. It’s surrender with a spreadsheet attached.

Look at the wire. Look at the panic. Look at Josh Cochran over at JUICE sitting there fondling his little first-round rookie pick like it’s a security blanket. That ain’t a strategy. That’s an admission of failure stamped in ink before the season’s even got a pulse. You think hoarding pieces gets you a trophy? You think shipping off a real asset just so you can draft some kid who hasn’t taken an NFL snap is going to save your soul?

While you’re out here panicking, trading away proven killers like Christian McCaffrey or CeeDee Lamb just to chase ghosts three years down the road, men with iron in their blood are trying to win today. Will Minick over at Dirty Magic gets it. He’s building to crush you right now, not five years from now when your mortgage is paid off. Will runs this league right, and if you’ve got something to say about how he runs it, you can shut your mouth and set a lineup.

I gave you the blueprint back in August. SHMEATtheBOY, Dirty Magic, Winterfell Whitewalkers. Those are the ones carrying the torch while the rest of you crawl into a hole and talk about your rebuilding windows. A rebuilding window is just a window you’re looking out of because you’re too scared to step into the ring.

I’ve been sitting in this chair since twenty-twenty-three. Three years running, I called the champion before a single snap was played. Topeka Cosbies in twenty-three. AthensAssholes in twenty-four. Macon Muskrats in twenty-five—yeah, Griffin Bear took my ink, hoisted the trophy, and forgot how to say thank you, but the pick is the pick. I don’t miss. I don’t hedge. I don’t hide behind a calculator like Marcus Vail or write love letters to last-place rosters like Frankie Doyle.

You want to talk about process? Your process is garbage. Your patience is an excuse. The season starts now, and if your team is already packing it in for next April, do the rest of us a favor and leave the keys on the counter.

Put some value in your chest. Week 1 is here. Suit up or ship out.
