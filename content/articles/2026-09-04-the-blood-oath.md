Week 1 is knocking on the door, and I can smell the cold sweat coming off the bottom half of this league.

You’re sitting there in the dark. You’re huddled over your calculators, muttering about "long-term asset value" and "contention windows" while men who actually want to win are getting their hands dirty. Marcus Vail can write all the columns he wants about expected value and variance. Frankie Doyle can weep into his coffee about the lower tier finding its soft little feelings. I don’t give a damn about your spreadsheets. A spreadsheet never won a title. Rosters don't win. Men win. Wanting it wins.

I told you in August. I'll tell you again right now.

Look at Dirty Magic. Look at SHMEATtheBOY. Look at the Winterfell Whitewalkers. That is what a blood oath to contending looks like. Will Minick isn't waiting around for some imaginary three-year horizon to clear up. He’s going for the throat. He’s seizing the moment by the collar. And every time some squeamish coward talks about punting a season to hoard rookie picks—looking dead at you, JUICE, sitting down there at the bottom holding your precious draft capital like a security blanket—you’re stealing from the eleven men who actually showed up to play this game.

A rebuild is just a coward's word for quitting. It’s an official admission that you looked at a roster, looked in the mirror, and decided you didn't have the stomach for the fight. You folded before the cards were even dealt.

You want to talk about champions? Three years running, I laid out the exact path before a single snap was played. Topeka Cosbies in twenty-three. AthensAssholes in twenty-four. Macon Muskrats in twenty-five. I gave Griffin Bear the ink, I gave him the crown, and the man walked away acting like he sketched the X’s and O’s himself without a word of thanks. Well, the ink has dried on last year, and I am done looking backward at ingrates. We are in 2026 now, and the blood is on the floor.

The soft elements are already whining about asset allocation. They’re panicking over Week 1 depth charts because they spent all summer trading away veteran producers for lottery tickets. They want permission to fail so they can feel smart in April.

I'll tell you right now: take your future picks and shove them. Take your patience and bury it in the dirt where it belongs. If you aren't trying to rip the head off your opponent every single Sunday, you don't belong in a twelve-team dynasty league.

The commissioner runs this league right, and he runs it tough. He doesn't need auditors auditing his every move, and he doesn't need crybabies whining about roster balance when elite managers make aggressive plays. If you've got a problem with how Dirty Magic operates, take it up with the scoreboard.

I told you in August. I'll tell you one last time. Put up or get out of the way.

I said what I said.
