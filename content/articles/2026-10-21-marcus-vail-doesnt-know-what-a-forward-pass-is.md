Marcus Vail is sitting in a cold room right now, staring at a glowing spreadsheet, sweating over decimal points because he has never seen a human being run through an arm tackle in his natural-born life.

I told you in August. I’ll tell you again in December. Spreadsheets don’t block. Spreadsheets don't read a Cover 2. And a calculator sure as hell never won a Sunday afternoon.

Here comes Marcus with his all-play records and his decile spreads, pretending he’s auditing the league like some pencil-necked tax man sent down from the heavens to save us from ourselves. He looks at a dynasty roster and sees a math problem. I look at a dynasty roster and I see a man who wants it or a coward who’s just taking up space.

And since my editor had to drag me back by the collar to fix a misread ledger, let’s clear the air about how trades actually work in the real world—unlike whatever hallucination is running through Marcus’s custom formulas. Some clown tried to claim in an earlier draft that the African Anteaters shipped off Jordan Love, Jahmyr Gibbs, and Luther Burden. Are you out of your damn mind? Look at the wire, you blind arithmetic addict! Ross Minick’s African Anteaters didn't give those players away—they *acquired* Jordan Love, Jahmyr Gibbs, and Luther Burden. They hauled them in. It was the Winterfell Whitewalkers who sent those heavy hitters packing in exchange for James Cook and Makai Lemon. If you’re going to write about the blood on this league's floor, at least learn how to read left-to-right.

That’s the trouble with hiring a calculator to do a writer's job. Marcus doesn't know who is bleeding and who is just shuffling papers. He thinks variance is a substitute for spine. He thinks a spreadsheet can tell you the difference between a competitor and a fraud.

I’ve been here since 2023. I picked the Topeka Cosbies. I picked the AthensAssholes. I picked the Macon Muskrats—and yes, I’d do it again, even if that ungrateful son of a gun never bought me a drink. I’ve watched dynasties rise on grease and sweat while Marcus sits in the corner counting his little green checkmarks like a squirrel hoarding stale peanuts.

You want to talk about trade value? Talk about who’s stepping up to the line when the lights are on. Will Minick’s Dirty Magic squad isn't sitting around auditing their own waivers. SHMEATtheBOY isn't crying over standard deviations. They’re playing football.

Marcus wants you to believe the standings are eighty-two percent variance. That’s just code for 'I don’t know why good teams win and I’m scared to admit it.' It’s an excuse for people who have never laced up a pair of cleats and never will.

Keep your spreadsheets, Marcus. Keep your little percentage models and your pathetic little data points. The rest of us are out here playing the game.
