They’re calling the hotline. They’re sliding into the DMs. *Wolf,* they say, *the Macon Muskrats are sitting at two and five. The defending champion is bleeding out in the mud. Are you going to throw him a rope? Are you going to save GriffinBear like you did last August?*

Let me tell you something about salvation. Salvation isn’t a charity case. Salvation is a contract.

In 2023, I called the Topeka Cosbies. Correct. In 2024, the AthensAssholes took the throne just like the ink said they would. Correct. And last year? Last year I walked down into the red clay of Macon, Georgia, I tapped GriffinBear on the shoulder, and I said: *That’s your champion right there.* I anointed them. I built that man a throne out of sheer force of will and a blank sheet of digital paper. I put the gold on his neck.

And what did I get?

Did I get a nod? Did I get a thank-you note? Did Griffin come walking through the press box with a tip of the hat to the longest-tenured writer this league has ever seen, the man who has correctly picked the champion three years running?

Not a damn word.

He walked around this league all year like he drafted the roster in his sleep, like he invented dynasty football, like the Howlin' Wolf hadn't prophesied his entire existence into the history books. He took my genius and he wore it like an off-the-rack suit, pretending he bought it himself.

Well, look at the standings now. Look at the ledger. Two wins. Five losses. Ten-forty-two points scored and ten-forty-eight bleeding out the back. A rotting carcass floating down the river, and everybody’s looking at me with big sad eyes asking why I won't reach down and pull him back to shore.

They want a straight answer. They want a formal breakdown of his roster construction, his trade portfolio, his depth chart efficiency. They want Marcus Vail’s sterile little spreadsheet metrics to explain why the Muskrats are collapsing.

I won't do it. I have pointedly refused to anoint the defending champion for 2026, and I will not explain it in plain terms. I will circle it until my lungs give out. 

Because a man who forgets his maker is just a tourist with a rented ring. 

GriffinBear wants to carry himself like a heavyweight while his roster falls apart at the seams? Let him. Let him sit down there in the bottom half with the wreckage. Let him look at the 2-5 record and wonder why the magic left the room the second he decided he didn't need the man who wrote his name in ink.

Marcus Vail can sit in the corner with his calculators, crying about variance and expected wins. Frankie Doyle can write another soft-focus love letter to whatever bottom-feeder just lost by fifty. I don’t give a damn about variance. I care about will. And Griffin lost his the day he thought he was bigger than the column.

You want a champion? Look at the top. Look at Dirty Magic. Look at SHMEATtheBOY. Look at the Winterfell Whitewalkers riding high while the pretenders drown in their own pride. 

I told you in August. I’ll tell you again when the snow hits the wire.
