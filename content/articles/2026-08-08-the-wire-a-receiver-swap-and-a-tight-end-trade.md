Nobody's played a game yet. Every record in this league currently reads a flat 0-0-0, and there's not a single thing in the standings worth writing a sentence about. Doesn't mean nothing's happened. By my count there have been eight completed trades already, which for a league that hasn't kicked off a single week is more than I expected to be writing about this early. Let's go through it.

The headline move is between Winterfell Whitewalkers and Macon Muskrats. Whitewalkers sent Garrett Wilson to the Muskrats. In return, Bryce gets back Luther Burden and Macon's 2028 first-round pick. Whitewalkers also sent their own 2027 second the other direction to close it out.

Read plainly: the Muskrats gave up a rookie receiver and a future first to bring in a proven, established target. The Whitewalkers gave up that proven target to bring in a younger one and stack an extra future first on top of what they'd already have. Neither side did anything you could call a mistake. They just wanted different things on the same afternoon, and the math lined up well enough for both of them to say yes.

The other trade worth its own paragraph is smaller — Vic Blends and AthensAssholes, tight end for wide receiver, straight up, no picks attached. Riley picks up Dalton Schultz. Jake gets Jayden Higgins going the other way. Neither team is saying much about it, which is usually a sign both sides think they got the guy they needed and don't feel like arguing about it in the group chat.

Everything else moved fast enough that I'm not going to pretend I can walk you through every asset with a straight face, but here's the shape of it. Whitewalkers and The Herbert Effect swapped a bigger package — receivers going both ways, two future first-round picks attached — which means Bryce has now been on the other side of three separate trades this week. Nobody in this league has been busier. Vic Blends and Buckhead Beavers traded quarterbacks, Dak Prescott for Jared Goff, with picks thrown in on top. Dirty Magic sent draft capital to AthensAssholes and got Ja'Marr Chase back for it. And Vic Blends and AthensAssholes ran it back for a second, separate deal after their first one.

The one I actually want to sit with is the three-team trade between Whitewalkers, Buckhead Beavers, and JUICE. Multiple 2026 picks changed hands across all three sides, and on the player end of it, Josh's team walked away with Michael Pittman, Kenny Gainwell, and Blake Corum.

I'll say the quiet part, gently, because somebody ought to. A team that's supposedly given up before August doesn't usually come out of a three-way trade with two more skill-position players and a running back added to the roster. That's not what tanking looks like. That's what building looks like when nobody's paying attention to it, which around here is most of the time. I'm not saying JUICE is good yet. I'm saying the moves so far don't match the story being told about them, and I'd rather point that out now than wait until December to say I noticed.

That's the wire for this week. Eight trades and not a single snap played. Ask me again once somebody's actually 0-1.
