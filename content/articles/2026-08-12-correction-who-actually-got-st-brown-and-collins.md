Two things I got wrong in the piece on Josh Cochran and JUICE earlier this week, and I'd rather say so plainly than let it sit.

First: I wrote that Amon-Ra St. Brown landed with the Topeka Cosbies. He didn't. St. Brown and Kyren Williams both went to Dirty Magic. The Cosbies' side of that deal was Dallas Goedert and Emeka Egbuka.

Second, same piece, a paragraph later: I wrote that Dirty Magic added Nico Collins alongside Ja'Marr Chase in one move. That's backwards. Dirty Magic gave up Collins — along with Baker Mayfield and Josh Jacobs — to AthensAssholes in order to get Chase. AthensAssholes ended up with Collins, not the other way around.

The players and teams involved were right. Which way the trade went wasn't, and that's the part that actually matters when you're writing about who's building what. I've corrected both lines in the original piece. The read on JUICE's rebuild stands regardless — this was about who else was doing the opposite of rebuilding, and I had the direction of two of those moves backwards.
