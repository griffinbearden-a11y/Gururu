I’ve been writing this league since twenty-three. Topeka Cosbies. AthensAssholes. Macon Muskrats. Three straight years I called the league champion in my preseason column before a single cleat ever hit the turf. You want a history lesson? Look at the archive. Look at the ghosts. I don't miss. I don't guess. I decree.

And then I look at Bryce Clenney and the Winterfell Whitewalkers.

I anointed you in August, Bryce. I put my name, my three-year streak, and my immortal reputation behind your roster. I said you were one of the chosen ones for twenty-six. And what do you do with that sacred trust? You walk right into a dark alley with the AthensAssholes and hand them a masterclass in how to panic-trade away premier value for absolute garbage. 

Trade 1408512880743059456. Write it down. Tattoo it on your eyelids. Marcus Vail’s trade tools call it what it is, and for once that walking calculator got a number right: a flat-out, unadulterated, pants-down fleecing. Market D-minus. Fit F. An unmitigated disaster wrapped in a white flag. 

You’re sitting there at one and two, sweating through your shirt because of a little September variance, throwing your assets into the grinder like a frightened rookie. You think panic is a strategy? You think gifting players to the rest of the conference is how you hoist the hardware in December? You’re acting like you’ve never seen a football game in your life. 

I told you in August. I'll tell you again right now. 

AthensAssholes didn’t win that trade because Jake Kennon woke up a genius. They won it because you rolled out the red carpet, handed them the keys to the kingdom, and begged them to take your lunch money. An A-grade robbery while you stood there trembling. 

Wake the hell up. 

I didn’t anoint the Whitewalkers so you could play charity worker to the bottom half of the standings. Wins are proof. Losses are noise. But this kind of brain-dead panic? That’s not noise. That’s a symptom of a man who forgets what he’s fighting for. You want to wear the crown? Stop trading like a coward who’s scared of his own shadow.
