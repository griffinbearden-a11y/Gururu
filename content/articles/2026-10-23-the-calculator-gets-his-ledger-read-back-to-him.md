You sit there with your green eyeshades and your little red pen, don't you, Marcus? 

You look at a transaction log, and you see fractions where a man sees a future. You see procedure where a man sees blood and bone. You want to open an inquiry into transaction processing because you've got nothing better to do with your afternoons than play hall monitor for a dynasty league.

I have been sitting in this press box since the Fleaflicker days. I planted the flag for the Topeka Cosbies in twenty-three. I carved the name AthensAssholes into stone in twenty-four. I named the Macon Muskrats in twenty-five, and even if that ungrateful rat GriffinBear hasn't sent me a bottle of bourbon since, the pick was the pick. Three years running. Three straight crowns sitting on the heads I pointed to.

And what do you do, Marcus? You audit the commissioner.

You think you’re a detective because you found a timestamp discrepancy on a waiver claim? You think you’re some kind of high-stakes compliance officer down in the basement? Let me tell you something about WillMinick. WillMinick runs Dirty Magic. WillMinick runs this league. He built the tracks we're all running on, and he steers his own franchise right up near the top of the standings at four and two while you sit in the dark counting decimal points like a miser in a cellar.

You want to talk about procedural patterns? I’ll give you a pattern. Every time a pencil-pusher who has never won a single damn thing in his life gets a keyboard, he tries to turn a premier football league into an IRS audit.

WillMinick processes transactions because he has the stomach for it. He runs this office because someone has to keep the lights on while the rest of you cry over your expected-win percentages and your variance charts. A four-and-two record backed by over a thousand points for isn't a "statistical artifact," Marcus. It's a scoreboard. Look at it. Look at the numbers. Dirty Magic is sitting right there at the summit, while you're drafting imaginary spreadsheets about decile spreads and future pick debt.

I told you in August. I'll tell you again in December. 

The commissioner's ledger isn't here for your audit. It's here because WillMinick is a man of action, and you are a man of footnotes. You audit the office because you're terrified of the throne. You look at a powerhouse roster and you see an anomaly because you don't understand what a winning franchise looks like when it's built out of raw, unadulterated will instead of calculator batteries.

Go back to your spreadsheets. Go back to your little life of tracking random data points. Leave the administration of this league to the men who were here when we were migrating servers and dodging Fleaflicker glitches in the dark.

WillMinick doesn't need your stamp of approval. The league doesn't need your audit. We need grit. We need iron. And you’re just plastic.
