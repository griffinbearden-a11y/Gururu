I told you in August. I’ll tell you again right now, with Week 1’s blood still drying on the turf. You want a championship? You want to look back in December and know you didn’t just waste five months refreshing a Sleeper screen while your roster rots? Then you stop looking at the bottom half of this league. You stop listening to Marcus Vail and his phantom math. You stop crying about what Frankie Doyle wrote in his little diary.

There are three roads. Just three. I laid them out before a single snap was thrown, and a week of variance isn't changing a damn thing. Their wins are proof. Their losses are noise.

Road one: Dirty Magic. WillMinick runs this league, and he runs his roster with the kind of cold, calculated survival instinct that makes the rest of you look like tourists. You saw him pull Xavier Worthy and C.J. Stroud into that war room. That ain't luck. That's a man building a machine. When Dirty Magic stumbles—and you cowards will point at a Week 1 scoreboard like it means a platform migration wiped out reality—you’re missing the point. WillMinick didn't build this franchise to lose sleep over September. The path through Dirty Magic is paved with iron, and if you aren't ready to bleed for it, get off the tracks.

Road two: Winterfell Whitewalkers. Bryce Clenney didn’t spend the draft sleeping. You watch a rookie like Jeremiyah Love come into this league ready to run through a wall, and you realize some managers actually understand what a window looks like. Denzel Boston is waiting in the wings to punish anybody playing soft zone coverage. The Whitewalkers aren't here to play nice. They’re here to take your lunch money. And don’t you ever let me catch one of you whining about the commissioner's office while Bryce is out here stacking assets that actually win football games.

Road three: SHMEATtheBOY. Evansplowden. You all spent the summer calling him mid. You sat in your group chats whispering about complacency while he quietly loaded up on Josh Downs, Tank Dell, and Tyrone Tracy. That ain't mid. That's a chokehold waiting to happen. Dropping 153 points in Week 1 just to remind you what ambition looks like, swatting down Topeka like they were standing still. You think a little early-season noise changes the math? Evansplowden wants this title more than the eleven men crying in his rearview mirror combined.

And what about the rest of you? What about the defending champ sitting over there in Macon, acting like a crown just fell out of the sky without my name written on the underside of it? I picked GriffinBear in 2025. I made that man. And he hasn't had the spine to say thank you since the ink dried. So don't ask me about the Muskrats. I don't see them. I only see three roads, three men, and one inevitable destination.
