I have sat in this press box since the ink was wet on Fleaflicker, and I am sick to death of watching frauds wear silk shirts they didn't earn. Three years running I picked the last man standing—Topeka in '23, Athens in '24, Macon in '25—and every single time I handed out a crown while pencil-pushing cowards like Marcus Vail counted their spreadsheets and wept into their keyboards. Now we are deep in the gravel-crunching middle of the 2026 season, and the standings are full of cheap furniture and rotting floorboards. You want a power ranking? I’ll give you the only list that matters: the men with the stomach for the blood, and the paper-shuffling cowards waiting to be put out of their misery.

1. Dirty Magic (WillMinick)
Will Minick runs this league right, and his squad is out here backing up every word I wrote in August with a 4-2 record that makes the rest of you look like Sunday school truants. You can whine about trades and commissioner bias all you want, but Dirty Magic is taking scalps while you're still reading waiver wire articles written by Frankie Doyle. This roster has iron in its spine, and until someone proves otherwise, the king sits where he belongs.

2. Shlomo Teittleman / SHMEATtheBOY (evansplowden)
They called him mid for two years, whispering in the dark because they didn't have the stones to admit he was building something real. I told you different, and Evansplowden is shoving those weak-ass narratives right back down your throats at 4-2. This roster has the stubborn grit that the rest of you soft managers trade away the second a shiny third-round pick dangles in your face.

3. Winterfell Whitewalkers (bryceclenney)
Bryce Clenney is stocking the shelves with young terror like Jeremiyah Love and Denzel Boston, refusing to fold when the board gets tight and the losses pile up. They’re sitting at 3-3, but the bones of this roster are forged in iron and built for January warfare. I anointed them in the preseason, and I don't retract a damn syllable.

4. loganmorris28 (loganmorris28)
Five and one on paper, sitting atop the standings like a rooster on a garbage heap while everyone else trips over their own shoelaces. Logan Morris managed to fleece Dirty Magic in a trade that sticks in my craw like a fishbone, but you can't argue with the scoreboard numbers. Let's see if this paper tiger actually holds up when the winter winds start howling through November.

5. Vic Blends (RileyParker)
Riley Parker clawed up to 4-2 with a roster that hits harder than a blues guitar string snapping in a thunderstorm. They traded with Macon and walked away looking like highwaymen who knew exactly which pockets to empty. Don't look now, but Vic Blends actually came to play real dynasty football this year instead of just paying dues.

6. Topeka Cosbies (Rickyshanks229)
My 2023 champion is sitting right at 4-2, quietly reminding everyone who laid the foundation of this damn dynasty while the rookies run around screaming. Rickyshanks229 doesn't need to holler from the press box because that championship ring from the Fleaflicker days does all the talking necessary. They’re a veteran problem waiting to explode all over your cute little Sunday lineups.

7. Buckhead Beavers (banks2002)
Banks and the Beavers are grinding out a 3-3 existence like a man shoveling gravel in a hurricane with a broken shovel. Historically a cellar-dweller of legendary proportions, this franchise is finally showing a pulse that makes me almost sick to my stomach to look at. Almost. Don't go buying championship banners for the doghouse yet, but at least they're breathing.

8. AthensAssholes (AthensAssholes)
My 2024 champion is currently scuffling at 2-4, looking like a tired ghost of the royalty they used to be when the world was young. Jake Kennon's crew is out here picking up Jordan Addison and trying to stitch a dead-on-arrival corpse back together with grocery store twine. A fall from grace is a terrible, beautiful thing to watch, unless you're the one holding the shovel.

9. The Irrelevants (beau1shirah)
Two and four, scraping the bottom of the barrel and picking up Brandin Cooks and Pat Freiermuth off the street like stray dogs looking for scraps. Beau Shirah is managing this roster with all the vision of a blind mole digging in a coal mine at midnight. You want respect in this league? Try winning a damn game that doesn't involve a coin flip.

10. Macon Muskrats (GriffinBear)
Two and four. The defending champion from 2025 is sitting right down in the mud where they belong after I handed them the golden ticket last August and didn't even get a nod of thanks. Griffin Bear took my genius, won a title, and started strutting around like he built the engine himself without my greasy fingerprints all over the block. Well, the engine’s seized up solid at 2-4, and I’m fresh out of pity for ingrates.

11. African Anteaters (RossMinick1)
One and five, dragging their pathetic carcass through the dirt while the Minick family name takes a serious public beating every single weekend. Ross Minick's squad couldn't stop a nosebleed if you handed them a roll of medical gauze and a Sunday morning prayer. This is what happens when you treat dynasty like a yard sale for unwanted parts.

12. JUICE 🧃 (Joshtcochran)
Two and four, holding the first rookie pick and treating it like a badge of honor instead of a scarlet letter of total organizational failure. Joshtcochran is the absolute baseline of what it means to quit on your roster before the opening whistle even blows. You want a measuring stick for how to ruin a franchise from the top down? Look right here, folks.
