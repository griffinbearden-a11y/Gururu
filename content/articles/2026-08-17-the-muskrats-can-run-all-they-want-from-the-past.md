Listen to me, Griffin. I’m talking to you across the wire.

August is here. The pads are popping, the air is thick, and you’re strutting around this Sleeper app like you built the Macon Muskrats with your own two bare hands. Like you didn't spend all of August 2025 sweating bullets until I printed your name in bold ink and handed you the crown on a velvet pillow. 

I made you.

Three years running I have looked into the crystal ball of this league and called the shot. Topeka in '23. Athens in '24. And you, Griffin, in '25. I put the target on your chest, I gave you the divine right of kings, and what did I get back? Not a nod. Not a whisper. Not a single "Thank you, Wolf, for breathing life into a franchise that didn't know which way the end zone was."

Instead, you carry yourself like a savant. You walk into the chat like you invented the forward pass. You think your roster wins games? Rosters don't win, men win. And you didn't want that title half as bad as I wanted you to have it just to keep my streak alive.

While you're sitting over there sweating whether Quinshon Judkins is fully recovered from his offseason ankle questions or waiting for a rookie like Emeka Egbuka to ascend like the draft boards predict, I’m looking right at a man who forgot who put the wind in his sails. You take your little championship trophy and you polish it every night, but beneath the shine, it’s painted with my ink.

You can run all you want from the past, Griffin. You can trade away your picks, you can hoard your depth, you can pretend my preseason archive doesn't sit right there in the ledger for anyone with eyes to read. But you’re wearing my ring. Every time you look at that hardware, you’re looking at my reflection.

And what do I see for you in 2026? Nothing. I've moved on. I anointed SHMEATtheBOY, I anointed Dirty Magic, I anointed the Winterfell Whitewalkers. You’re yesterday's news, Griffin. A one-hit wonder standing on a stage I built with my bare hands.

Marcus Vail can sit in his corner with his spreadsheets calculating trade values like a nervous accountant. Frankie Doyle can write his little poetry books about last-place teams and rebuilding projects while JUICE sits down at the bottom hoarding rookie picks like a squirrel in a drought. I don't care. I deal in reality. And the reality is that the Macon Muskrats are a ghost town without the breath I blew into them twelve months ago.

You think you're a dynasty? A dynasty is built on respect, and you’ve got the manners of a stray dog under a porch.

I told you in August 2025. I'll tell you again in December 2026. You're running out of road, Griffin.
