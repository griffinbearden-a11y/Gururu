I told you in August. I'll tell you again right now, sitting here in the teeth of October.

Half this damn league is walking around with their pockets full of unproven rookie draft capital like terrified squirrels hiding nuts for a winter that never comes. You want a shiny college kid who hasn't taken an NFL snap? You want to trade away a grown man who plays football on Sundays for a piece of paper that says *maybe*?

That’s not dynasty. That’s cowardice. That’s a confession written in crayon.

Look at the wire. Look at what these boys are doing. Hoarding picks like they’re buried treasure while the actual games are being played right in front of their blind faces. You think a fifth-round dart throw is going to keep you warm when Dirty Magic or SHMEATtheBOY or the Winterfell Whitewalkers come rolling down the track? You think some rookie receiver is going to hand you a title?

I was here in 2023 when I handed the Topeka Cosbies their crown. I was here in 2024 when AthensAssholes took the throne just like I wrote. I was here in 2025 when the Macon Muskrats—and yes, I said it, I built that man's championship out of Georgia clay before he ever learned to say thank you—claimed the whole damn thing. Three years running. Three rings called from the pulpit before a single cleat hit the turf.

And what were the losers doing while I was prophesying? Trading away production for future picks. Punting seasons. Stealing from the eleven guys who showed up to actually compete.

You look at a team sitting at the bottom holding a top rookie pick like it's a badge of honor. It’s not an asset. It’s an admission of failure. It’s a white flag waving in the wind. You want to draft Xavier Worthy or C.J. Stroud or whoever the hell the talking heads are hyping this week? Fine. Go ahead. Build your museum of *might-be*. Meanwhile, men who want to win football games are trading for proven muscle.

Marcus Vail can sit in his corner office crunching his little spreadsheets until his eyes bleed, trying to tell you that future draft equity is the cornerstone of long-term value. Vail has never won a damn thing in his life. He wouldn't know the smell of a championship trophy if it bit him on the ankle. Frankie Doyle is out there writing poetry about last-place teams like they're stray dogs needing a warm meal. They're not stray dogs. They're quitters.

A roster doesn't win. Men win. And a man who trades away a running back like Aaron Jones or Isiah Pacheco for a future draft pick is a man who is terrified of the scoreboard.

The rookie pick market is a scam run by cowards for cowards. You want to build a dynasty? Put your chips on the table today. Stop buying lottery tickets. Stop waiting for tomorrow.
