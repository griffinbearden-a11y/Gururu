This is why your roster construction resembles an unmitigated accounting disaster, because draft capital is not a lottery ticket you can discard without penalty, but rather the principal asset backing your entire franchise's long-term solvency.

When a manager views future picks as expendable currency rather than amortized liabilities, they fail to comprehend that every traded-away asset accelerates the steepness of their eventual decline when the aging curve finally flattens their roster.
