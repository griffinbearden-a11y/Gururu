Three years. Three picks. Three rings.

I told you about the Cosbies in '23. I told you about Athens in '24. I told you about the Muskrats in '25. You can go look it up — except you can't, because two of those columns don't exist anymore, and I'm not doing this again with you people. The pick is the pick. I felt it before the season and I said it before the season, and if the archive ate the paper trail that's a filing problem, not a credibility problem. Ask me again and we're done talking.

Here's 2026.

**Dirty Magic.** WillMinick runs this league right and he builds a roster the same way. No wasted moves. No cute rebuild talk. A team that wants it, built by a man who's earned the right to want it. I don't need to explain this one twice.

**Winterfell Whitewalkers.** Bryce doesn't talk much and doesn't need to. This is a team that shows up. You want to find me a hole in this roster, go ahead and look. Take your time.

**SHMEATtheBOY.** Evan gets called "mid" by people who've never built anything themselves. I'm not interested in the label. I'm interested in the roster, and the roster says otherwise. Write that down, because you're going to see it again in December and I don't want to hear that you're surprised.

That's the three. That's the whole list. Anybody not on it is not walking through that door this year, and I don't care how loud you get about it in the group chat.

You'll notice a name missing. You'll ask about it. I'm not going to explain it to you — not this column, not the next one, not when you bring it up at the trade deadline like it's a gotcha. A man won a title off a pick I made and never once said thank you for it, and I don't owe him a second one. Figure that part out yourselves.

And don't ask me about the team with the first rookie pick. That's not a story, that's a confession. A man tells you who he is when he gives up before August, and I already know who that man is. Beneath comment. Stays beneath comment.

Two more things, since apparently I have to say them out loud every year.

One — Marcus Vail got hired this season to sit in the back and count. That's fine. Count away. Counting isn't picking, and picking is the job. I'll be here in December either way.

Two — Frankie Doyle is going to write you eleven hundred words this year about why some 2-and-8 team is actually fine, actually building something, actually has a plan. Read it if you want. I'm not covering the bottom of this league and I'm not starting now. Somebody's got to write about the teams that are actually trying to win.

Last thing, and I mean this the way I always mean it: I am not wrong about this. I was not wrong last year. Doesn't matter what page it's on. Doesn't matter if it's on any page at all. I said it, I'm saying it again, and come championship week you're going to remember exactly who told you first.

That's the pick. Don't ask me twice.
